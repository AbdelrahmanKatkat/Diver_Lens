      About Dive ColorLab

      A static, privacy-friendly underwater colour lab. Open photos and videos from your computer, correct them, and export the result. Nothing leaves your device.

      Supported: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF (first frame) and browser-playable video (MP4, WebM, MOV on some browsers). RAW and HEIC are not decodable by browsers.

      Keyboard shortcuts

      • Ctrl+Z undo   Ctrl+Y redo
      • Hold \ to see the original
      • W pick a grey / white point   Esc cancel
      • Space play / pause video

      Tips for better results

      • Set a custom white balance on the camera, or shoot RAW, when you can.
      • Red is almost gone below about 15 m. Auto can only recover what the camera still recorded.
      • Everything runs on this device; your files are never uploaded.
