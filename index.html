      About Dive ColorLab

      A static, privacy-friendly underwater colour lab. Open photos and videos from your computer, correct them, and export the result. Nothing leaves your device.

      Supported: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF (first frame) and browser-playable video (MP4, WebM, MOV on some browsers). RAW and HEIC are not decodable by browsers.
